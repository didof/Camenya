# Preserve Takes and trim through metadata

A finalized Take movie remains immutable because rewriting it would turn a reversible cleanup decision into destructive media editing and would weaken Camenya's recovery guarantees. Audio analysis may persist a Trim Suggestion, but only an explicitly approved Take Selection affects Timeline playback and Project Export; both consume that retained range directly, without creating an intermediate trimmed movie, while the Original Range remains available until the Take itself is deleted.
